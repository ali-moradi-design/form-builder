New field types: `url`, `phone` (with `normalizePhone`), `time`, `dateTime`, `rating`, `nps`, `slider`, `hidden`, and the display-only `heading`, `paragraph` and `divider`. `allFields` includes them; `richRenderers` and `allRenderers` draw them. In the focus layout hidden fields and dividers get no page of their own.
